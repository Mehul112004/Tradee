## Fixed differences from strict v1

| Measurement | Balanced context proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Hourly trend | Price beyond EMA200 by 0.25 hourly ATR; EMA20 above/below EMA50 with at least 0.25 ATR separation; EMA200 five-hour slope has the intended sign; ER24 >=0.30. Do not additionally require EMA50 beyond EMA200 |
| Hourly range | ER24 <=0.25; absolute EMA200 slope <=0.01 ATR/hour; rolling 24-hour width between 4 and 10 hourly ATR; two confirmed hourly pivots per boundary within 0.50 ATR, anchored in the last 48 hours and separated by at least three hours |
| EMA rejection slope | Intended sign of the execution EMA200 slope, without the additional 0.01 ATR/bar magnitude minimum |
| Persistence and geometry | Two consecutive hourly snapshots; immediate transition veto on mismatch; all original frozen-box migration checks, first touch/approach/confirmation rules and 0.75–2.5 ATR stops remain |

All original dull/shock/unknown-data vetoes, crossing caps, expiry/drift, event/funding windows, target barriers, fees, slippage, net reward/risk and finite-capital loss limits remain. Range middles and countertrend entries remain excluded. Costs will not be reduced to obtain more entries.

The purpose is to test whether redundant trend alignment and exceptionally narrow range-boundary evidence prevent meaningful sampling. These are unvalidated heuristic cutoffs. A larger sample can still have negative expectancy or unacceptable range-transition losses. Select only on earlier training using the frozen minimum of 30 resolved modeled entries, positive mean and the same drawdown-penalized score. Insufficient evidence means abstain; no automatic quick-flow/default/release change follows this experiment.

All variants also use the same frozen strict-v1, point-in-time **audit regime** for time-in-regime marked P&L. Their own strategy regimes remain separate.
