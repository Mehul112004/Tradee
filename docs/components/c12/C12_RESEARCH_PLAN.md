# C12 — chronological validation and experiment registry

Implemented 19 October 2026. C12 owns research orchestration; C10 and C11 do not import it. See [implementation](C12_IMPLEMENTATION.md), [verification](C12_VERIFICATION.md) and [upgrade register](../../platform/COMPONENT_UPGRADES.md).

## Dependencies and consumers

| Input | What C12 receives | What uses it inside C12 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C01/C02 through frozen C11 manifests | Universe/metadata versions, availability mode, immutable datasets and observation cutoffs | Source isolation, reproducibility, identical-data stress checks |
| C03/C08 through C11 | Feature definitions, strategy identity, candidate inventory, decision prefixes | Protocol matching, complete opportunity denominator, neighborhood comparisons |
| C06/C07/C09 through C11 | Funding evidence, fee/delay assumptions, fills, label intervals, causal outcome revisions | Purging, mature/unknown coverage, cost stress and net-R evidence |
| C11 | Complete immutable simulated outcomes and source provenance | Revision-aware C10 training/evaluation, stress label joins |
| C10 | Earlier-only snapshots, broad-cohort fits, frozen predictions and inference abstentions | Inner selection, outer/final fits and later-window admission |
| C15, later | Forward degradation and replacement evidence | A new counted experiment; never a silent revision of an old release |

C13 receives a frozen research policy/model and validation evidence; its shared-capital evaluation remains necessary. C14/C15 receive research release identity and outstanding gates. C12 never issues orders or Telegram messages.

## Research decisions

1. **Freeze the complete procedure.** An experiment pins every variant's C11 run IDs **and report content hashes**, plus estimator settings, rationale/parameter description, historical/manual trial count, prior research disclosure, family-wide trial budget, inner/outer/final windows, purge gap, selection objective, uncertainty settings and evidence gates. Registration also pins all Tradee Python source and the Python runtime. TBot-used or otherwise inspected data is development data.
2. **Consume completed measuring-instrument outputs.** Generate each source with C11 `simulate run`; C12 orchestrates actual C10 training and evaluation over those pinned outputs rather than implementing another simulator. Each variant has one strategy/policy/cost protocol; multi-family C11 reports must first be replayed into separate single-protocol runs. Symbols and C10 cohorts may be multiple. Distinct strategy families may compete only if the manifest deliberately declares that search. C02/C11 manifests include store-local event references, so run IDs alone do not identify outcome bytes across independent databases. C12 requires `source_report_hashes`, verifies full C11 inventories against them, and checks them even on cached development retries. Holdout content checks happen only after the access journal.
3. **Select inside earlier history.** For every outer fold, fit each predeclared base variant on its inner training window(s), score its disjoint earlier inner tests, and select the passing variant with greatest selected net R per inventoried candidate. Ties use lexical variant name. Persist this selection before scoring the outer window. C10 filters outcome revisions by the training cutoff; integrity checks can read the complete pinned development artifact inventory, so model-input isolation within development is logical rather than file-level blinding. Holdout sources are physically separate and remain unread by C12 until access is logged. A fixed sole hypothesis needs no artificial inner search. The final pre-holdout policy repeats exactly the declared inner procedure on final development history; it does not choose the best outer performer.
4. **Use actual label and knowledge clocks.** C10 selects the last outcome revision observed strictly before the training cutoff. C12 additionally excludes decisions at/after `test_start - purge_gap`, and closed label intervals ending on/after that boundary. Unknown/unresolved earlier labels stay in coverage and cannot train C10. No post-test training observations are allowed; there is therefore no post-test embargo in this exclusively forward chronology. The pre-test gap is explicit rather than inferred from bar count.
5. **Own opportunities by detection.** Test windows are half-open, chronological and disjoint. Earlier source history provides feature warm-up; outcomes may mature in a declared tail. A candidate is counted once in its detection window, including if its position extends into another fold. Resampling moves all symbols in a calendar block together and checks that the block duration covers observed selected label spans.
6. **Measure a research utility, not funded return.** For a window, `utility = sum(net R of eligible mature candidates) / count(all inventoried candidates)`. Abstention contributes no selected position and zero selected utility. An eligible outcome that is unresolved, unfilled or absent under stress makes utility/interval unknown and fails evidence. This is not expected portfolio return, ROI or Sharpe. Report conditional mature mean R and estimation/admission coverage alongside it.
7. **Require independent time evidence.** Use the full predeclared test windows, retaining empty edges and untested inter-window calendar gaps without treating them as observed trades. Use circular calendar blocks, exact decimal sums, a fixed RNG seed and nearest-rank percentile bounds. Keep cross-asset rows together. Allocate each reported interval's tails using `alpha / (2 × frozen family trial budget)`, including stress variants and declared historical trials. Use rational tail ranks, including nonterminating decimal probabilities, and refuse too few draws to resolve the tail. Intervals condition on the frozen predictions and observed outcomes; bootstrap draws do not refit or reselect models and do not quantify full uncertainty over the training/search process. This is approximate block-bootstrap inference under dependence/stationarity/representativeness assumptions; no exact finite-sample or simultaneous-all-windows coverage claim is made. Family accounting cannot repair undisclosed adaptive data reuse.
8. **Check robustness after freezing the winner.** Predeclare neighboring base variants and adverse fee/slippage/delay variants. A stress preserves hypothesis, funding policy, quantity, feature definitions, candidate policy and exact source data. Reuse the baseline predictions/admission and join independently resimulated stressed outcomes. Do not refit under stress. Missing selected fills/labels fail conservatively. Neighbors get their own earlier training and later diagnostic evaluation; their outer results cannot change the winner. Include fold attribution and leave-period/asset-out utility checks. Every declared stress/neighbor attached to the selected winner is evaluated.
9. **Make final access explicit and irreversible in the registry.** Development never reads holdout run manifests or outcomes. It may not use sources with decisions or revision tails later than holdout start. After passing development, freeze final selection/model, then journal `holdout_opened` before reading holdout evidence. A failure leaves access consumed; only the identical frozen job can resume. Reserve holdout periods globally in this single-market registry, preventing resealing through another experiment/family and development overlap with reserved periods.
10. **Keep failure and rejection.** Atomic immutable job artifacts, a hash-chained audit journal, counted family reservations and cached completed jobs make failures resumable. A process crash leaves a running job that can retry; a failed evidence result is a completed rejected result, not an execution failure to discard. Changing source/runtime, variants, thresholds or windows requires a new counted experiment and a new untouched holdout. Completed release decisions remain immutable.
11. **Separate research from live approval.** Passing an attested uninspected holdout yields a frozen `research_candidate` release, with exact model/protocol/evidence references. Previously inspected/unattested holdouts remain diagnostic and always reject research promotion. All releases, including rejected ones, say `live_approved: false`; C13/C14/C15 and audited representative real data remain required.

## Primary research and limits

The official [TimeSeriesSplit documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.TimeSeriesSplit.html) describes ordered splits and an optional sample gap. Tradee requires an additional implementation based on event-specific label intervals and actual revision availability; the library split alone does not supply those contracts.

The [arch time-series bootstrap documentation](https://bashtage.github.io/arch/bootstrap/timeseries-bootstraps.html) describes circular, moving and stationary block resampling. Tradee uses a declared fixed circular block length; it does not automatically discover a valid dependence length or prove stationarity. Block/threshold sensitivity requires additional predeclared variants, not selecting the most favorable setting afterward.

The original [PBO paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf) motivates measuring selection over a family of configurations. The [DSR paper](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf) addresses performance inflation from selection and nonnormal returns. Neither statistic is implemented on irregular candidate R: C13 must first provide a defensible regular shared-capital return series. The complete trial ledger, nested chronology, family tail allocation and untouched final window are implemented now; specialized PBO/DSR diagnostics remain conditional extensions.

C12's holdout gate controls its own API, not access to C11 databases, raw files or human memory. Truthful inspection/history disclosures and a single durable registry are prerequisites. Existing precomputed but uninspected C11 runs are supported; pre-registering unknown future dataset hashes with a later controlled attachment workflow is a future extension. Declared C11 scope coverage is not proof that the underlying feed is continuous or representative. Synthetic E2E outcomes verify engineering, not market profitability.
