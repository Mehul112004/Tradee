# C14 — prospective paper decisions and Telegram lifecycle

Implemented 20 October 2026 as a bounded **shadow/paper-test baseline**. C14 owns orchestration and notification state. C11 remains a measuring instrument and never imports C14. See [operation](C14_IMPLEMENTATION.md), [verification](C14_VERIFICATION.md) and [remaining work](COMPONENT_UPGRADES.md#c14-live-signals-and-telegram).

## Scope and release boundary

C12 releases and C13 receipts retain `live_approved: false`; a C13 receipt also retains `promotion_authorized: false`. C14 honors those contracts. Supported modes are `shadow` and `paper_test`. Configuration cannot enable actionable `live` mode. A deployment requires a passing `research_candidate`, the exact compatible portfolio recipe, matching source content and producer/runtime, and a paper session beginning strictly after final evidence/model availability.

The model's actual training-source run/report is verified before using its frozen scenario, fees, execution, funding and candidate configuration. One release/model/scenario is supported; assets and direction remain those of that frozen scenario. Multi-release composition and full portfolio-policy inference remain C12/C13 gates. Existing releases generated by older Tradee source digests must be regenerated under a new counted experiment; C14 does not rewrite their provenance to make them load.

**Paper-fill notices are informational.** C13 currently admits at the tested first executable C09 opportunity, after the configured processing/delivery/manual delay. A notice created from that observed paper fill is not a prospective instruction to enter again. Sending such a notice and adding another human delay would change the tested protocol. Pre-entry actionable messages therefore require a separate alert/reservation/delayed-entry policy, timing measurements and C12/C13 validation. The baseline message explicitly says `PAPER TEST — NO TRADE INSTRUCTION` and distinguishes paper fills from user fills.

## Prefix scheduling and state

Each tick captures a recorded C02 snapshot through a supplied cutoff. Warm-up and a bounded session/tail are explicit; strategy decisions belong to `[start,end)`. The complete source prefix is replayed through the shared C11 and C13 implementations, including closes, funding revisions, simultaneous asset allocation and regular paper equity. Rebuilding prefixes avoids an unverified alternate live feature/exit engine. It trades performance/storage for reproducibility; unlimited full-rate Binance operation is not established.

A committed watermark contains source count, cursor and actual event-content prefix hash. A later tick must retain that prefix exactly; an additional observation at or before the closed cutoff is refused. Normal backfills/revisions recorded later retain their later availability. A small configurable source-settle delay helps avoid reading a partially committed same-millisecond batch; it does not prove a feed is complete. A changed past paper admission or fill also refuses publication.

Signals retain `detected`, `suppressed`, `expired`, `paper_open`, `paper_closed` or `paper_unresolved` phase. Delivery has a separate lifecycle. One stable signal ID binds deployment, candidate, policy, tested quantity and horizon across changing dataset vintages. An old signal cannot be recreated merely because the next snapshot has another dataset hash.

C14's paper book tracks the frozen hypothetical policy even when notification is paused, missed or rejected. Notification acceptance does not create a user position, debit cash a second time, or establish receipt/execution. Pausing suppresses pending notices; resuming never resurrects old entries. This paper-policy benchmark remains separate from eventual voluntary user-fill recording.

C11/C13 analysis artifacts publish first in their own immutable stores. C14 then commits its cycle, shared paper account, signal phases, outbox changes and audit events in **one C14 transaction** under compare-and-swap. A failed C14 publication can leave unreferenced analysis artifacts, but cannot leave a partial signal/notification intent. No cross-database atomicity is claimed.

## Freshness and send eligibility

A new paper-test notice is queued only on first observed admission, with an open paper position, valid current thesis, healthy feed/book and acceptable measured analysis latency. Its expiry is:

`min(candidate_entry_expiry, first_paper_entry + max_notice_age_ms)`.

Catch-up after downtime records historical paper outcomes and stale suppression; it does not send old entry instructions. Global feed health checks fresh quotes, marks and complete candles for the configured symbols. C13 supplies current risk/funding coverage; C10 rechecks model applicability and age.

Before claiming HTTP work, the dispatcher rechecks the latest committed cycle and source evidence. Trades or marks that trigger exits/partials, late or gapped trigger paths, changed marks on exposed instruments, candles, settlements and due paper timers require another tick before a send. Ordinary in-range trades and unchanged marks do not starve the send check. Changed mark paths are replayed through C13 so an intermediate equity peak/drawdown cannot be lost. New quote observations can be checked directly with shared risk. C07 receives freshly recomputed C03 evidence bound to that current immutable C02 vintage; C10 keeps the original candidate's feature proof. This distinction prevents cross-dataset feature substitution and is regression-tested.

The new C07 price, quantity and fee must still equal the tested first-entry economics. Changed economics, invalid thesis, unknown/stale state or failed expectancy suppress the notice. This conservative paper-notification gate does not model a later alternative entry. The original signal, C10 estimate, C07 fill, research identity and absolute expiry remain in the message/evidence.

## Telegram transport and delivery semantics

The official [Telegram Bot API](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#sendmessage) defines HTTPS `sendMessage`, a bounded text body and an accepted Message response. C14 uses plain text without Markdown/HTML parsing, a fixed Telegram host, verified TLS, no redirects, no automatic library HTTP retry and bounded response reads. A positive receipt must include the expected chat, exact text and a positive message ID. It proves service acceptance, not human receipt or a user trade.

Telegram's documented [`retry_after`](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#responseparameters) controls flood-wait retries. The [official FAQ](https://core.telegram.org/bots/faq#my-bot-is-hitting-limits-how-do-i-avoid-this) describes per-chat/group/broadcast constraints. C14 persists per-bot and per-chat throttles across sessions in one database, defaults to 3.5 seconds per chat and 100ms per bot, and honors a longer server wait. It does not use paid broadcasts. Independent databases/hosts or other senders are outside that shared throttle.

`sendMessage` does not expose a caller idempotency key. Consequently the application cannot promise exactly-once remote delivery. A stable signal reference helps reconciliation but cannot make Telegram deduplicate requests.

| Transition | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Pending/retry → in flight | Persist attempt token, lease, frozen message, fresh guard and throttle reservation before HTTP |
| Confirmed acceptance | Retain `sent` and message ID; never automatically resend |
| Explicit 429 rejection / demonstrably unestablished connection | Schedule bounded retry after server wait/backoff/throttle, subject to expiry and attempt budget |
| Permanent client rejection or TLS verification failure | Retain `failed`; no blind retry |
| Timeout, lost response, malformed response, server/redirect uncertainty | Retain `ambiguous`; no automatic retry |
| Process loss after durable claim | When the lease expires, retain `ambiguous` even if the notice is now expired |
| Late verified receipt for the same unresolved attempt | Record acceptance and whether it arrived after expiry; do not create another attempt |
| Manual reconciliation | Explicit evidence plus `sent`/message ID or definitive `not_sent`; no automatic inference from silence |

An expired pending notice never starts HTTP. Request timeout is bounded by remaining notice life. A remote server may still accept after a deadline; the result is recorded honestly rather than hidden or retried. A manual `not_sent` assertion can only return to retry while still valid, unpaused and within its attempt budget. Uncertain absence should remain ambiguous.

Credentials come only from `TRADEE_TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` at an explicit send invocation. The numeric bot/chat destination is frozen in a paper-test deployment; a mismatched token bot ID is refused. Tokens, request URLs and provider exception strings are not persisted in messages, operational artifacts or returned errors. Structured reason codes replace transport exceptions. No token is copied from TBot and no external message is sent by automated verification.

## Operational acceptance and remaining gates

The foreground watcher reuses C01/C02 stores, ticks paper state, recovers leases, optionally dispatches paper tests, and reports health. Ctrl-C stops it; no daemon or external schedule is installed. Source/candidate/artifact/cycle/byte/session limits fail without truncating history. Code/runtime changes require a new frozen deployment/research lineage.

The baseline records source cutoffs, actual audit-record times, analysis duration, send attempts, HTTP elapsed time and late receipts. A representative live host/stream load benchmark, collector repair/funding cadence, process supervision, backup/restore drill, authenticated user-state handling, scale/migrations and C15 monitoring remain required operational work. Full-prefix rebuilding can hit limits quickly on active quote/trade streams; choose a bounded study and measured capture plan rather than infer 24/7 readiness from synthetic tests.

Acceptance for this increment: deterministic production replay, atomic paper/signal/outbox state, frozen research/content checks, fail-closed freshness, tested concurrency, restart/timeout/expiry reconciliation, raw-input E2E and a repeatable opt-in manual connectivity check. It establishes engineering behavior, not a real-market edge, actionable live authorization or actual Telegram visibility.
